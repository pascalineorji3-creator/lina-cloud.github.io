Bloomie

An accessible child-development reading companion designed to make stories, emotional support, and inclusive communication feel natural for autistic and deaf or hard-of-hearing children.

Product in development
01 · Overview

A reading experience designed around the child, not around the interface.

Bloomie is a founder-owned product concept that combines children's reading, accessibility, emotional support, and parent-guided experiences into one calm digital environment.

The product is being designed specifically for children who may experience communication, sensory, emotional, or accessibility barriers during ordinary digital reading experiences.

Rather than treating accessibility as an additional setting, Bloomie places inclusive communication and emotional safety inside the core experience.

02 · The Problem

Accessibility should be part of the experience from the beginning.

Communication barriers

Many digital children's experiences are primarily designed around spoken or written communication, leaving children with different communication needs underserved.

Sensory overload

Excessive movement, visual noise, sound, or interaction can make an otherwise useful experience difficult to navigate.

Emotional needs

Reading can intersect with difficult emotions. Bloomie therefore treats emotional regulation as part of the product experience rather than an unrelated feature.

Parent visibility

Parents and caregivers need appropriate visibility without turning the child's experience into a surveillance-heavy interface.

03 · Product Concept

Two familiar companions. One calm environment.

Bloomie uses two recurring companions, Pete and Mia, to create continuity across the reading experience.

The companions are intentionally designed to remain ageless and approachable rather than feeling overly childish, allowing the experience to remain appropriate as a child grows.

Pete

A familiar companion designed to support the reading and interaction experience through expressive communication.

Mia

A second companion providing another recognizable presence throughout Bloomie's experiences.

The companion system is designed around consistency, emotional safety, and accessibility rather than constant stimulation.

04 · Inclusive Communication

Communication is designed to happen in more than one way.

Bloomie is designed to support both spoken communication and sign-based interaction, including SEE and ASL support.

Speech Spoken companion interaction designed to accompany the reading experience.
Sign language Companion signing designed around SEE and ASL accessibility.
Facial expression Companion expressions provide additional emotional context without relying exclusively on text or speech.
Mouth movement Companion animation is designed to coordinate visual mouth movement with spoken interaction.
05 · Reading Experience

Reading is the centre of the product.

Bloomie is fundamentally a reading companion rather than a generic entertainment application.

The surrounding features are designed to support the child's relationship with stories instead of competing with them.

06 · Emotional Safety

The product makes room for emotions without overwhelming the child.

Bloomie includes an emotional check-in designed to provide a simple way for a child to communicate how they are feeling.

Emotional check-in

A simple interaction designed to help the child communicate their current emotional state.

Calm Corner

A short, approximately one-minute breathing experience designed to provide a calmer transition when a child needs a pause.

Parent-only insights

Selected emotional information can be surfaced to the parent or caregiver rather than exposing adult-facing information inside the child's interface.

Low stimulation

The visual and interaction system is intentionally designed to avoid unnecessary movement, noise, and visual overload.

07 · Bloom Garden

Progress becomes a gentle visual experience.

Bloom Garden is designed as a calmer progression layer where the child's engagement with Bloomie can translate into a growing, personalized environment.

The goal is not to turn reading into a competitive game. The interaction is intentionally softer and more reflective.

Bloom Garden is designed to reinforce engagement without creating pressure, streak anxiety, or excessive reward mechanics.

08 · Parent & Therapist Ecosystem

Adult controls stay separate from the child's world.

Bloomie's architecture separates the child's experience from parent- and therapist-facing functionality.

Parent Zone

A dedicated adult-facing space for appropriate insights, settings, and account-level controls.

Content workflow

Parent and therapist story submissions can follow an approval process before becoming available inside the product.

Admin moderation

Content is intended to pass through an administrative review workflow before publication.

Private / public content

Content architecture is designed to distinguish private experiences from approved content that can be made available more broadly.

09 · Technical Architecture

A mobile-first product with a separate administrative layer.

Bloomie's planned technical architecture separates the mobile experience, backend services, and administrative environment.

Mobile application React Native + Expo for iOS and Android.
Authentication Firebase Authentication.
Database Firebase Firestore for application data and structured user/content records.
Storage Firebase Storage for supported application assets.
Backend logic Firebase Functions for server-side workflows and controlled application logic.
Admin environment A separate Next.js administrative dashboard with Firestore listeners for content and operational management.
10 · Content Safety

Children's content requires a controlled publishing workflow.

Bloomie's content model is designed around moderation and approval rather than unrestricted user publishing.

Submission Parent or therapist content can enter a controlled submission workflow.
Administrative review Submitted content is intended to be reviewed before publication.
Asset restrictions The planned workflow restricts uploaded story imagery to approved cartoon-style content.
Visibility controls Content can be designed for private or approved broader availability.
11 · Accessibility & UX Principles

Calm interaction is a product requirement, not decoration.

Low stimulation

Reduce unnecessary animation, visual noise, and interaction complexity.

Predictability

Familiar companions and consistent interaction patterns help reduce unnecessary cognitive load.

Multiple communication modes

Support spoken, visual, signed, and expressive communication rather than assuming one mode works for everyone.

Child-first interface

Adult controls and operational information remain separated from the child's core experience.
