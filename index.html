03 — AI Evaluation

I look beyond the answer.

AI evaluation asks more than whether a response sounds convincing. It considers whether the response follows instructions, uses evidence appropriately, addresses the actual question, and communicates uncertainty or limitations.

◎

Instruction Following

Examining whether an AI response addresses the requested task, respects stated constraints, follows the requested format, and avoids irrelevant output.

✓

Factuality & Evidence

Checking important factual claims against appropriate sources, identifying unsupported statements, and separating evidence from assumptions.

⇄

Comparative Evaluation

Comparing responses using consistent criteria such as accuracy, relevance, completeness, clarity, usefulness, and adherence to the task.

⌕

Error Identification

Looking for contradictions, reasoning gaps, missing information, misleading confidence, and answers that fail to resolve the original problem.

≋

Output Quality

Assessing readability, organization, relevance, and whether the response provides a practical answer without unnecessary repetition or unsupported certainty.

!

Limitations & Uncertainty

Identifying what a response cannot establish, where verification is needed, and whether limitations are communicated clearly.

Digital Access Across African Countries

How does internet use differ across six African countries? This case study compares reported internet-use rates in 2023 to highlight the differences and communicate what the available data can—and cannot—tell us.

49.5 percentage points Difference between the highest and lowest reported rates

What the case study covers

  • Comparison of six African countries
  • Visual presentation of public data
  • Interpretation of key differences
  • Data sourcing, methodology, and limitations

The analysis distinguishes reported internet use from affordability, connection quality, and equal access.
