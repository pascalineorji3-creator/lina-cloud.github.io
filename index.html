02 — AI Evaluation

I don't just use AI. I evaluate what it produces.

My evaluation work focuses on structured comparison, factual accuracy, instruction following, data quality, usefulness, transparency, and identifying limitations in AI-generated analysis.

Structured Evaluation

I use explicit criteria and scoring rubrics to assess AI responses instead of relying only on subjective impressions.

Fact & Evidence Checking

I check whether numerical claims, interpretations, and recommendations are supported by the available information.

Model Comparison

I compare outputs from different AI systems on the same task while keeping the evaluation criteria consistent.
