Lina Engine: A living professional identity.

Lina Engine is an AI-powered professional identity platform designed to transform a person's work, projects, skills, and professional evidence into a continuously evolving public professional presence.

Product in development
01 — Overview

Beyond the traditional portfolio.

Traditional portfolios often require professionals to manually decide what to display, write descriptions, update projects, organize evidence, and maintain multiple disconnected profiles.

Lina Engine was designed around a different idea: instead of treating a professional website as a static collection of pages, treat professional identity as a living system that can discover, organize, update, and present evidence of a person's work.

02 — The Problem

Professional evidence is often scattered.

Projects may live on GitHub. Designs may exist in creative tools. Documents may live in cloud storage. Professional information may be spread across social profiles, workspaces, repositories, certificates, and client work.

Lina Engine explores how these disconnected sources can become structured professional evidence without requiring the user to manually rebuild their identity every time something changes.

Fragmented Evidence

Work, projects, skills, achievements, and professional information often exist across different systems.

Manual Maintenance

Traditional portfolios depend heavily on the owner repeatedly writing, editing, organizing, and updating content.

Limited Context

A static portfolio can struggle to present different aspects of a professional's experience for different visitor purposes.

Lost Opportunities

Valuable professional evidence can remain disconnected from relevant opportunities and potential collaborators.

03 — Product Concept

A professional identity that can evolve with the person.

Lina Engine is designed as a professional identity layer rather than simply another portfolio template.

The system can organize evidence into a professional presentation while keeping the owner in control of what is reviewed, edited, and ultimately published.

05 — AI Discovery

Turning existing work into professional evidence.

Connected workspaces can provide the raw material from which the system identifies projects, technologies, categories, achievements, descriptions, and other professional signals.

Discover

Identify potentially useful professional information from connected sources.

Structure

Organize discovered information into professional entities such as projects, skills, services, and evidence.

Review

Give the owner an opportunity to inspect and refine AI-generated discoveries.

Publish

Only reviewed and approved professional information becomes part of the public identity.

06 — Two Experiences

One professional identity. Two carefully separated experiences.

Owner Experience

  • Manage professional identity
  • Connect workspaces
  • Review AI discoveries
  • Edit generated content
  • Control publication
  • Manage visibility
  • Configure AI features

Visitor Experience

  • Explore professional evidence
  • Navigate relevant sections
  • Use optional AI guidance
  • Discover projects and capabilities
  • Interact with professional content
  • Access opportunities where appropriate
07 — Visitor Intelligence

Helpful guidance without taking over the experience.

Lina Engine's visitor intelligence concept is deliberately non-intrusive. Visitors can choose what they are interested in, allowing relevant sections to move upward in the existing layout without forcing the visitor through a separate navigation flow.

Optional AI Guide

AI guidance can remain available without constantly interrupting the visitor.

Purpose-Based Exploration

Visitors can indicate what they are looking for, such as projects, skills, services, or professional experience.

Session-Aware Journey

The experience is designed to avoid repeatedly showing the same guidance when a visitor pauses and resumes their journey.

Layout Preservation

Personalization changes the order of relevant content rather than forcing visitors into a completely different website.

08 — Opportunity Engine

Connecting professional evidence with possibilities.

The Opportunity Engine is designed to use the professional identity and evidence already organized inside Lina Engine to identify potentially relevant opportunities.

Professional Matching

Match opportunities against professional capabilities, projects, services, and evidence.

Evidence-Aware Discovery

Use actual professional evidence as context instead of relying only on a manually written profile.

Opportunity Categories

The broader concept can support opportunities such as jobs, freelance work, collaborations, research, grants, competitions, and other professional opportunities.

Owner Control

Opportunity features remain configurable by the professional rather than being forced into the public experience.

10 — Privacy & Cost Design

Intelligence should not mean unnecessary AI calls.

A major design consideration was avoiding a model call every time a visitor opens a page or interacts with the professional identity.

Instead, the architecture favors precomputation, caching, owner-side generation, and stored results wherever possible. This reduces unnecessary inference costs while also limiting how much visitor behavior needs to be processed dynamically.

Precompute

Generate reusable AI-derived information when the owner's data changes rather than repeatedly generating identical results.

Cache

Store reusable outputs so visitors can access relevant information without requiring a fresh model call.

Read-Time Efficiency

Favor database reads, stored state, and deterministic logic for ordinary visitor interactions.

Privacy-Aware Recognition

Return recognition and journey-state concepts are designed with privacy considerations rather than relying on unnecessary personal identification.
